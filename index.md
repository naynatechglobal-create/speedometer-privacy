# Privacy Policy for GPS Speedometer: HUD & Trips

Last updated: 3 November 2026

**Who we are.**
GPS Speedometer: HUD & Trips ("the app") is published by NaynaTech, Portsmouth, United Kingdom. Contact: naynatechglobal@gmail.com.

**Location.** 
The app uses your iPhone's GPS to show your speed and to record trips you start. Location is used only while the speedometer screen or HUD is open, or while a trip you started is running. Your location, speed and trip routes are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Subscriptions.** 
The app is sold as a subscription through Apple. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier for your app installation, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device information such as the iPhone model and iOS version. It does not receive your name, email, location or trips. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Payment itself is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

**No accounts, no ads, no tracking.** 
The app has no sign-up, shows no ads, and contains no advertising or analytics trackers. We do not sell or share personal data.

**Crash reports.** 
If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These contain no location or trip data.

**Your rights.** 
Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask what data we hold about you and ask for it to be deleted. Because the app keeps no account and no server-side data about you, the only data we could hold is the anonymous subscription record described above. Email naynatechglobal@gmail.com with any request.
